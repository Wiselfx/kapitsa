Structured output 2/3: sampling under grammar masks in the engine: the first token, plain steps, DFlash2/KFlash verify rows and batched passes (4B: a reply under a JSON schema decodes as fast as the same tokens unconstrained, 168 vs 167 tok/s with KFlash and 90 vs 90 without; unconstrained prefill and decode unchanged against origin/main, the same replies)

GPU: logit_mask_rows (src/kernels/sample.metal) sets a row's logits of the tokens its mask leaves out to -inf before
the top-k stage, so every sampler and acceptance after it reads the masked distribution unchanged: greedy is the
argmax over allowed tokens, sampling draws from the allowed tokens renormalized (after top-k / top-p), DFlash2's exact
speculative acceptance and KFlash's tree acceptance see masked rows. enc_logit_mask (src/model/logit_mask.cpp) is called
by every sampler a constrained reply uses (Qwen35 enc_sample and verify rows, encode_verify_multi per group, Nemotron-H
and Dory steps); with no masks on the sequence (Seq::mask_rows 0) it encodes nothing. A pass whose masks the host has not
written yet waits on a shared event: the host computes them while the pass's layers run and signals it.

Engine: the prompt's last chunk samples the first token under the grammar's first mask. With a DFlash2 drafter each
cycle verifies under masks: after the drafting half, the host computes every verify row's mask from the drafted chain or
tree (row r: the grammar's state after row r's path) while the verify runs, so the reply is masked plain decoding's.
Batched passes do the same per member (batch_cycle from the chains, batch_plain_run pass by pass). Other drafters run
the reasoning in their loop and stop at </think>; the formatted part decodes as masked plain steps (pipelined: the next
step is queued before the host reads the token). Reasoning stays free: the grammar starts after </think>. Masked cycles
do not feed KFlash's budget (its calibration is saved and shared; the grammar's rejections would skew it).
NI_GRAMMAR_SPEC=0 takes masked plain steps with DFlash2 too.

tools/logit_mask_check (GPU, small sizes first): masked rows against the CPU, the event wait, greedy and sampled draws
after a mask at Qwen's vocabulary size. Run with MTL_SHADER_VALIDATION=1 MTL_DEBUG_LAYER=1: 33 of 33, no validation
error.
